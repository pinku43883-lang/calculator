About Search Pro

Welcome to Search Pro, your advanced search solution powered by Google Custom Search.

Features include:

  • Powerful Google-powered search
  • Complete search history tracking
  • Dark mode for comfortable browsing
  • Easy history management
  • Responsive design for all devices

Your privacy is important to us. All history data is stored locally on your device and never shared with any third parties.
